## Description

This page contains noticeably little text. Search engines rate very “thin” pages
as unhelpful because they offer barely any information.

## Why it matters

- Pages with substantial, original content rank considerably better.
- Many near-empty pages (e.g. auto-generated) can drag down the quality of the
  whole domain.

## How to fix it

Add helpful, original content that genuinely answers the page's question
(process, details, common questions, examples). For many data-generated pages:
provide enough real value per page, or consolidate similar pages.
