## Description

This page's slug matches another one except for one or two characters (`contact`
vs. `contcat`, `product-overview` vs. `products-overview`). That suggests a typo
or two addresses that are very hard to tell apart.

## Why it matters

- If one of the two addresses is a typo, it looks unprofessional and is hard to
  remember or share.
- Very similar URLs are easily confused by visitors and complicate maintaining
  internal links.
- If both pages are related in content, duplicate content becomes a risk too.

## How to fix it

Check whether this is a mistake or two legitimate pages:

- For a typo, correct the slug and catch the wrong address with a redirect.
- If both pages are intended, choose clearly distinct, descriptive slugs so users
  and search engines can tell them apart reliably.

Numbered series (`part-1`, `part-2`) are deliberately not reported.
