## Description

Typical placeholder text was found on this page (e.g. “Lorem ipsum”, “TODO”,
“insert text here”, “sample text”). Such leftovers come from authoring and should
not be online.

## Why it matters

- Dummy text makes the page look unfinished and unprofessional to visitors.
- Search engines may rate the page as low quality or incomplete.

## How to fix it

Replace the placeholder with the real content, or take the page offline until it
is finished. Also check templates/archetypes (`archetypes/`) in Hugo so new pages
do not start with dummy text again.
