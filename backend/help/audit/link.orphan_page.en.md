## Description

Not a single internal link from another page in the project points to this page.
It is therefore “orphaned” – reachable only by those who know the exact address.

## Why it matters

- Search engines find pages mainly through links; orphan pages are crawled rarely
  or not at all.
- Users do not come across the page while navigating normally.

## How to fix it

Link the page where it belongs thematically: from the navigation, an
overview/category page, or from related posts. A sitemap entry alone does **not**
replace internal linking.
